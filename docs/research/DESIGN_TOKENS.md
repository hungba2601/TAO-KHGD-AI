# Design Tokens - EduPlan AI (KHGD AI VIP V4.0)

## Colors
- **Primary / Brand Accent**: Amber/Orange gradient (`#f59e0b` to `#ea580c`)
- **Background Light**: Slate-50 (`#f8fafc`)
- **Background Dark / Sidebar**: Slate-900 / Slate-950 (`#0f172a` / `#020617`)
- **Text Primary**: Slate-900 (`#0f172a`) / White (`#ffffff`)
- **Text Muted**: Slate-500 (`#64748b`)
- **Card Background**: Pure White (`#ffffff`) with border `rgba(226, 232, 240, 0.8)`
- **Success / Badge**: Emerald-500 (`#10b981`)
- **Warning / Alert**: Amber-500 (`#f59e0b`)
- **Danger / Delete**: Rose-500 (`#f43f5e`)

## Typography
- **Primary Font**: `Inter`, sans-serif (Google Font)
- **Document Body Font**: `Times New Roman`, serif (Vietnamese Government Standard)
- **Monospace Font**: JetBrains Mono / font-mono (for terminal logs)

## Spacing & Radii
- **Card Radius**: `rounded-3xl` (24px) / `rounded-2xl` (16px)
- **Button Radius**: `rounded-xl` (12px) / `rounded-full`
- **Shadow**: `shadow-md shadow-amber-500/20`
